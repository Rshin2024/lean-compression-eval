# Dataset provenance and redistribution

Library candidate contexts and proof excerpts are derived from the Lean community's Mathlib project at commit `274ed6d67d9b226c557813b9ce437574cccdce11`. Source paths, declaration names, source lines, and hashes are retained in each task's provenance. Original authorship notices in preserved source contexts remain applicable.

Mathlib is distributed under Apache License 2.0; the full license is included in `MATHLIB_LICENSE`. Reconstructed contexts and evaluator target names are modifications for this evaluation, not upstream Mathlib files.

Fresh statements and initial reference proofs were drafted by Codex and checked with Lean; they are not outputs from the evaluated model conditions. They are evaluation-specific combinations and modified assumptions. Their origin label does not establish that equivalent statements were absent from any model's training data. Public manifests intentionally omit their private reference proofs.

The collection is a candidate dataset until its explicit audit gates pass. Source compilation alone does not establish target isolation, absence of equivalent lemmas, metric closure, difficulty balance, or disjoint mathematical families.
