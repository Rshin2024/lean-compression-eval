These are historical audits supporting the paper’s formatting and metric-mechanism discussion. Exact original/export hashes are in `export-manifest.json`. The parse-failure audit retains aggregate counts and diagnostic summaries, but its sampled raw submission excerpts are omitted. Separate submitted-proof files and downloaded literature caches are not included.

They require excluded private response/proof-graph records; they are not portable statistical replay entry points. Use `../reproduce.py` and the notebook for the published data replay.
